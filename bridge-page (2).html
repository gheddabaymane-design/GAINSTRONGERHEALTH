The lymphatic system

The lymphatic system is a network of vessels and nodes that works alongside circulation to help manage fluid balance and support normal immune function.

Unlike the heart, it has no central pump. Everyday movement helps lymph move through the body, which is one reason gentle activity and regular position changes are often part of a balanced routine.
