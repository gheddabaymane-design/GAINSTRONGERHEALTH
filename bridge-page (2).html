The 2-Minute Morning Drink: Apple Cider Vinegar, Lemon & Baking Soda

Updated September 2026 · 4 min read · Kitchen Wellness Daily Editorial Team

If you found this page through a video or social post, here's the full recipe as promised. This simple morning drink takes about two minutes to prepare and has become a regular part of many people's morning routines.

The morning drink, prepared with apple cider vinegar, lemon, a pinch of baking soda, and optional cucumber and mint.

What you need (serves 1)

  • 1 glass of water (room temperature or warm — not boiling)
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar (ideally unfiltered, "with the mother")
  • Juice of half a fresh lemon, or 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 small pinch of baking soda (about ⅛ teaspoon)
  • 3–4 cucumber slices (optional)
  • A few fresh mint leaves (optional)

How to make it

1

Pour the water into a tall glass.

2

Add the apple cider vinegar and lemon juice, then stir gently.

3

Add the small pinch of baking soda. It will fizz — wait about 10 seconds for it to settle.

4

Stir in cucumber and mint if using. Some people prefer to drink it a little while before breakfast, though there's no set rule — timing is a matter of personal preference.

A note on safety: Use no more than ⅛ teaspoon of baking soda. Drinking through a straw and rinsing your mouth with plain water afterward can help, since lemon and vinegar are acidic and may affect tooth enamel over time. If you take medication or have a health condition, it's worth checking with your doctor before adding this to your routine.

Some people find this drink a pleasant part of their morning. Others find the tartness takes getting used to, and preparing it fresh every day — measuring the baking soda carefully each time — is an extra step that doesn't always fit a busy schedule.
